##Unfinished.

Make weapons upgradeable, so that they can do more damage.

##Finished.

A game pays a little more gold, 15 for each kill and 5 for each headshot, so weapons are a bit easier to get, and both can be changed in save.json.
The weapon stats in save.json are the real ones and count when you change them: the magazine, the range, the damage and the price, which the shop and the inventory read out, with the range in metres.
After buying a weapon, the screen reader says "Purchase has completed", the same words as the window.
In the weapon test range, two zombies that reach you at once on your last heart kill you instead of leaving you below zero hearts.
A volume in settings.json written with an unusual digit, such as a superscript two, no longer stops the game from starting.
The game no longer plays its recorded voice, because everything it said is spoken by the screen reader, and the recordings are kept out of play in an unused folder.
A zombie that has grabbed you no longer takes a heart while the game is paused, and its grab does not carry on after a restart.
Pausing just before you die holds the death, so restarting no longer ends the new game with a game over.
Escape pauses in your first game after the tutorial instead of going back to the menu.
Restarting from the result panel after your first game starts the game again, where you can walk and pause with P, instead of putting you back in the tutorial's first lesson.
The voice over row is gone from the main menu, so the game is driven entirely by the screen reader.
A purchase is no longer announced twice at the same time, because the recording is gone and only the screen reader says "Purchase has completed".
The tutorial is spoken by the screen reader, with the keys or controller buttons for each lesson, and a lesson that sends in a zombie waits for Enter instead of a timer.
All of the game's speech goes through the screen reader instead of the recordings, and changing weapon says the weapon's name.
The result panel no longer reads a number over the next row, because the screen reader says each row and its number in one go and stops when you move, so there are no recordings left to overlap.
The zombies at 10:30 and 1:30 are heard closer to 12 o'clock, so it is easier to tell which lane a zombie is in.
Games are free, so there are no coins to wait for, and the coins row is gone from the main menu.
A game controller plays the whole game: the stick and D-pad aim at the lanes, the buttons reload, change weapon, shake and pause, it vibrates for hits, and a menu row turns the vibration off.
The MG80 fires continuously while its key is held, and the AK47 still fires one shot per press.
